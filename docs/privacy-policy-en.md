# MenuBarCalendar Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 7, 2026**

MenuBarCalendar ("the app") is a macOS menu bar calendar tool. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle and use your information.

## 1. What We Collect

### Calendar Event Data
After you grant permission, the app reads calendar event data from Apple Calendar (Calendar.app) on your device to display events for the selected date in the menu bar panel. **All calendar data is processed entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server or shared with any third party.**

You can revoke permission at any time via System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars. After revoking, the app will not read calendar events, but other features like the date display in the menu bar will continue to work.

### Network Requests
The app queries a third-party public API (`timor.tech`) to fetch Chinese public holidays and adjusted working days. This request **does not include any of your personal information** (no calendar data, no name, no device identifier) — only the year parameter (e.g., `2026`) for lookup. Holiday data is cached locally and used only to mark holidays on the calendar.

You can turn off holiday display in settings; after doing so the app will not make this network request.

### Preferences
The app saves your preferences locally (e.g., date display format, week start day, panel style). These are stored only in `UserDefaults` on your device and are **never uploaded or shared**.

## 2. What We Don't Collect

- ❌ No name, email, phone number, or other identity information
- ❌ No location data
- ❌ No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
- ❌ No calendar event content uploaded to any server
- ❌ No usage tracking

## 3. Data Storage & Security

All app data is stored inside the macOS App Sandbox, following Apple's sandbox security mechanism. Uninstalling the app automatically removes related local data.

## 4. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
